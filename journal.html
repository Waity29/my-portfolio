Rivan IT Cebu

Rivan IT Cebu is an information technology company that provides software development, IT solutions, and technical services to support businesses in improving their digital operations. The company focuses on delivering practical, efficient, and innovative technology solutions tailored to client needs.

UP in IT Cebu

The University of the Philippines (UP) Business Incubation Program for Information Technology supports innovators and startups by helping transform IT-based ideas into viable businesses. It provides mentorship, technical support, and access to research and industry networks, enabling the development and commercialization of software and digital solutions while fostering entrepreneurship and innovation.

Dynata Philippines Inc

Dynata is a global data and market research company that provides reliable consumer insights through online surveys, data analytics, and research solutions. The company supports businesses by delivering high-quality data to aid informed decision-making.

MATA technologies

MATA is a virtual reality map provider of tourist destinations in the country. Deriving its name from the Filipino word "mata", which means eyes, Mata Tech aims to give a 360° preview of real estate properties and notable destinations.

T.A.R.S.I.E.R. 117

T.A.R.S.I.E.R. 117 is an emergency response and disaster management unit established by the provincial government of Bohol to coordinate rescue operations and disaster relief efforts in the province.
